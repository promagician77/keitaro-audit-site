What the audit covers

⚙

Server & Infrastructure

Version, ClickHouse, disk, queue, cron, SSL — the foundation everything else rides on.

🌐

Domains

SSL on every tracking domain, domain-to-campaign ratio, clean DNS records.

📊

Campaigns & Flows

Flows present, default catch-all, offer assignment, weight distribution, naming conventions.

🔗

Offers & Postbacks

HTTPS enforcement, S2S postbacks, click-ID macros, dead-offer detection, network diversity.

🤖

Bot Filtering

Bot flow exists and is first, custom IP/signature lists, proxy detection, bot-traffic percentage.

🎯

Traffic Filters

GEO, device, ISP, connection type, geo-database currency, uniqueness.

📈

Performance

Conversion rate, ROI tracking, landing health, click limits, LP click tracking, timezone consistency.
